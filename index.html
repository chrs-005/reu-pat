How attendance changes the quest

Check Absent next to a person's name. Empty starting spots are filled by separating an intact starting pair, choosing the last originally listed duo first. The second name in that duo moves into the vacant starting spot, using that spot's clue and initial location.

When no whole duo is left to divide, a starting spot stays vacant. If this creates seven starting units, the unit opposite the vacant spot skips Round 1 and receives its Round 2 clue immediately. Skipped meetings have no envelope to plant.

All five teams retain their original rosters and meeting instructions. Mark everyone present to restore the default plan. Your choices are saved in this browser; export backups before moving devices.
